## Large binary inventory (2026-08-15 hardening audit)

| Artifact | Size | Classification | Why kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| `models/deployed/tabular_model.joblib` | 0.8 MB | **Required for production** | Final champion regressor, served by `/predict` |
| `models/deployed/tabular_pipeline.joblib` | <0.1 MB | **Required for production** | Feature pipeline + tuned params |
| `preprocessed/embeddings_{resnet18,resnet50,dinov2_vits14}.npz` | ~72 MB total | **Required for reproducibility** | `src/satellite/embeddings.load_cache`; lets the multimodal/fusion phases re-run **offline** without re-fetching tiles or re-extracting features |
| `preprocessed/train_clean.pkl`, `test_clean.pkl` | ~3.6 MB | **Required for reproducibility** | Clean cached frames; `load_clean_train`/test run without the gitignored raw `data/*.xlsx` |
| `models/vision/phase2*/head_*.pt` | ~3.2 MB | Research provenance | Experiment A/B head checkpoints for the archived DL extension |
| `models/deployed/vision_price.pt` | 43 MB | Research provenance | TorchScript archive of the evaluated vision model; **not served**; retained as the historical negative-result evidence |
| `reports/figures/gradcam_*.png` | ~2.4 MB | Research provenance | Grad-CAM evidence for `reports/project_report.md` §11 |
| `reports/val_predictions_best_tabular.csv` | 0.09 MB | Intermediate / regenerated | Written by `scripts/error_analysis.py`; consumed by archival scripts only |

No tracked binary was removed in the hardening pass; every large artifact is either
deployment-required, reproducibility-required, or research provenance. If external hosting
is ever desired for the 72 MB embedding cache, it is fully regenerable via
`python scripts/extract_embeddings.py --encoder <name>` (requires Mapbox tiles for the 2,189
covered properties, or the committed `preprocessed/image_manifest.csv`).
